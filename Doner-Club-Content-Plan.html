01 Campaign Overview

Objective

Introduce Doner Club to the Hyderabad market, build curiosity around the new outlet, establish product awareness and encourage customers to visit and experience the brand.

Target Audience

Young adults (18–35), college students, young professionals, food enthusiasts, friends and families looking for casual dining, and customers interested in international and quick-service food experiences.

Key Communication Objectives

  • Create awareness about Doner Club's arrival in Hyderabad.
  • Introduce Döner as a distinctive food experience.
  • Showcase products, preparation and restaurant ambience.
  • Build trust through genuine customer experiences.
  • Encourage first-time visits and repeat purchases.
