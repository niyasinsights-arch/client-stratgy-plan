Two Things To Flag

The mix leans entertainment-heavy

As arranged, the actual split is roughly Entertainment 6 · Product 4 · Lifestyle 3 · Brand & Heritage 3 — close to target, but hotter on entertainment than the recommended 30%, because your idea list simply holds more entertainment concepts. Moving one character format to a rest slot next month pulls it back in line.

No dedicated Sales / Conversion post in the 16

Your mix reserves 10% for Sales / Conversion, but none of the 16 is an explicit conversion piece. That fits the soft-sell strategy — if you want to hit the 10%, the ₹599 Sampler Box is the natural add, placed late (Day 25–31) once the emotional groundwork is laid.

On the GOCS conversions

Hooks, visuals and captions are written from your idea labels and the GOCS voice — I couldn't open the individual Pinterest/Instagram references, so treat each visual direction as a starting cut and adjust it against the actual pin.
