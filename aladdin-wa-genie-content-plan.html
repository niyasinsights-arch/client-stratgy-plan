Real video · 30 sec
Concept
Cinematic luxury ad with a well-groomed male model showing confidence, elegance and style through his interaction with the perfume.
Scenes
  1. Model adjusts his watch.
  2. Close-up of opening the bottle.
  3. Bottle held against the light.
  4. Applying on neck and wrist.
  5. Sitting on a wooden stool with the perfume.
  6. Product close-up and logo reveal.

Some things don’t need to be loud to be noticed. Elegance is found in the details. A fragrance becomes more than a scent. It becomes part of your identity, your style and your presence. Confident. Refined. Effortlessly elegant. Aladdin Wa Genie. Arabian Elegance. Everyday Luxury.

Minimal white/grey studio. Bearded male model, hair in a bun; black shirt, brown trousers, sunglasses, accessories. Soft dramatic lighting, slow motion and macro shots. Premium luxury instrumental.

Video 02 – Fragrance Is a Memory

Real video · 30 sec
Concept
Storytelling video where a male creator speaks about fragrance, memory, emotion and identity. Positions the brand as more than a perfume.
Scenes
  1. Model on a stool in a dark studio, speaking to camera.
  2. Close-up of him examining the bottle.
  3. Spraying the wrist, adjusting his collar.
  4. Silhouette near a window.
  5. Removing the bottle from premium packaging.
  6. Final product shot in natural light with logo.
Dialogue
Some people remember faces. I remember fragrances. I don’t wear perfume just to be noticed. I wear it for the mood, the confidence and the way it makes me feel. Because fragrance is more than a scent. It’s like emotions in liquid form. And sometimes, the strongest impression isn’t what people see. It’s what they remember after you’ve left. Aladdin Wa Genie. Arabian Elegance. Everyday Luxury.
Production
Dark minimal studio with a window. Male creator in black shirt and grey trousers. Props: stool, bottle, premium box. Warm cinematic light, shallow depth of field, soft atmospheric instrumental.

Discover the Collection.

Video 03 – The Case of the Unforgettable Fragrance

Real video · 30 sec
Concept
Humorous crime-investigation-style ad where a girl lying on the floor and two investigators turn the scene into a perfume promotion.
Scenes
  1. Girl on the floor, crime-scene tape reading “A SCENT THAT STAYS.”
  2. Investigators take photos and notes.
  3. Girl sits up and speaks to camera.
  4. Close-ups of the bottle, cap opening, spray.
  5. Investigators question her.
  6. She lies back down; cinematic product reveal.
Dialogue
Girl: “Everyone’s investigating my death, when they should be investigating how Aladdin Wa Genie smells this luxurious!”
Voice-over: “Subtle, classy and effortlessly luxurious. A fragrance that leaves an unforgettable impression.”
Investigator: “But how did you die?”
Girl: “Honestly, I don’t even remember. You guys figure it out!”

Minimal indoor set. Props: yellow tape, camera, notebook, perfume. Dramatic lighting, quick cuts, suspense sound effects, then luxury music.

3. Social content ideas (4)

Idea 1 – The Three Wishes

Reel · Brand awareness and engagement
Hook
“What if your perfume could grant you three wishes?”
Concept
A person opens a mysterious perfume bottle and a Genie offers three wishes, all tied to fragrance.
Scenes
  1. Mysterious bottle on a table.
  2. Person discovers and opens it.
  3. Genie appears in magical smoke: “You have three wishes. What would you choose?”
  4. Wishes appear: a fragrance that gives confidence; a scent of elegance; a fragrance that leaves an unforgettable impression.
  5. Showcase Legacy, Velour and Ember.
  6. All three bottles and the logo.
CTA
Which fragrance would you choose: Legacy, Velour or Ember?

4. Brand consistency checklist

  • Same bottle appearance and product details in every video, including the comedy and AI pieces.
  • Brand colours: burgundy, terracotta and gold, plus warm beige tones.
  • Premium lighting and cinematic presentation throughout.
  • Consistent logo and closing message: Aladdin Wa Genie. Arabian Elegance. Everyday Luxury. Where Fragrance Becomes Magic.
  • Clear product visibility and a relevant CTA in every piece.

5. Campaign goals

  • Build luxury brand positioning and improve product awareness.
  • Create emotional connections with the audience.
  • Increase Instagram engagement and highlight the fragrance collection.
  • Drive traffic and website purchases.
