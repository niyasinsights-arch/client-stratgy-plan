Experiential Campaign

The Fres N Co Healthy Challenge

A public fitness game taken to where the crowds already are — parks, beaches, walking tracks, colleges, IT parks and malls. Anyone can play, win a reward, and — most importantly — film it.

More store visitors More UGC reels Stronger brand recall Low cost · high shareability
The Concept

Move a little, win a bowl

Run a public health-challenge activity in high-footfall locations where people are already out and active. They join a simple game, complete a quick task, and instantly win a reward. Inclusivity is the key — a 30-second plank or 10 squats invites everyone.

Where to Set Up

Parks, beaches, walking tracks, colleges, IT parks, malls and busy public stretches. Match the spot to the audience. Carry a banner and QR code so onlookers can find Fres N Co even if they don't participate.

Challenge Menu

Let people choose their level — or let a spin-the-wheel assign the task for extra fun.

  • Easy — 10 squats
  • Easy — 20 jumping jacks
  • Medium — 30-second plank
  • Anyone — mini healthy habit challenge
  • Finale — spin-the-wheel for the reward

Reward Ideas

Everyone wins something — variable rewards make it addictive and re-postable.

  • 10% off coupon
  • Free add-on or topping
  • Buy 1 Get 1 on select items
  • Free healthy drink with order
  • Voucher for the next visit
  • Instagram feature + surprise gift for best participant
Run of Show

How to actually run it

1 · The Hook

Standee at the spot: "Do 10 squats. Win a meal. 👀" — curiosity stops passers-by mid-walk.

2 · The Challenge

Person picks or spins for a task. 30–45 seconds max.

3 · The Film

Host films it vertically — fun, energetic, reaction-led. This footage is the real product of the campaign.

4 · The Reward

Spin the wheel → instant win. Hand over the coupon with a genuine "come visit us."

5 · The Loop

Ask them to tag @fresnco and use the campaign hashtag. Reshare every tag to stories — that reshare pulls the next person in.

Why It Works

The strategic payoff

More Store Visitors

A fun reason to engage — and a voucher that pulls the visit to the café.

More UGC Content

Every participant becomes a content creator. A week of reels in one session.

Reels & Stories

Movement + reward + reaction is inherently watchable — exactly what the algorithm rewards.

Page Engagement

Tags, reshares and the hashtag loop spike comments and profile visits.

Brand Recall

People remember what they did, not what they read. Fres N Co becomes a physical memory.

Community Proof

Crowds seeing others play signals "this is a brand people love" — the trust shortcut.

How It Plugs Into the Content Plan

Tease it in pre-launch stories, run the activation after opening, and use the footage to feed your trust content, UGC reels and a story highlight under "Events." Boost the best participant reel with a geo-targeted Meta ad for cheap, high-relevance reach.

Experiential Campaign

Swap Your Sunday with Fres N Co

A surprise-invitation campaign. Coupons are dropped in public, high-footfall areas — and each one gives access to a special protein menu at Fres N Co. The coupon feels like a hidden pass, not a regular discount.

Core: Protein Menu Drop Offline surprise Exclusive menu access Weekend habit
Campaign Line

Swap Your Sunday with Fres N Co.
Find the Coupon. Unlock the Protein Menu.

Alternate: Swap Your Sunday. Choose Healthy. Choose Fres N Co.

Strategy Goal

What this campaign is built to do

Increase store visits, trial orders, and repeat customers by making Fres N Co feel like a fun, healthy, and shareable brand. The campaign creates curiosity in public spaces and turns the brand into a healthy weekend food habit.

Campaign Structure

  • Drop coupons in public or high-footfall locations
  • Make the coupon look like an exclusive healthy invitation
  • Protein menu available only to coupon holders
  • Clearly display protein value — 20g, 25g, or 30g
  • Free add-on for customers who redeem

Offer Design

  • Special protein bowl
  • Healthy Sunday menu
  • Free add-on with coupon
  • Limited-time weekend offer
  • Next-visit coupon for return trip

The menu should feel premium yet easy to understand — instantly healthy, protein-packed, and worth trying.

Content Plan

  • Show the coupon drop in Reels or Stories
  • Record people finding or receiving the coupon
  • Capture customer reactions when they visit
  • Film the food being prepared and served
  • Repost customer Stories and UGC

Visual Direction

  • Use green, white, and natural food colours
  • Showcase protein bowls and fresh ingredients clearly
  • Keep the coupon design simple yet premium
  • Bold typography for protein quantities
  • Sunday and weekend visual cues
