META STRATEGY · INSTAGRAM & FACEBOOK

Marshalway — 30-Day Meta Plan

A one-month organic + paid plan for the Smart Ironing Machine. Because the product is currently sold out, this month is designed to capture and warm demand now — building a waitlist and a retargeting audience — then convert hard the moment stock returns.

Prepared by Speakerbox  ·  Recommended investment: ₹75,000 / month (adjustable)  ·  Channels: Instagram + Facebook

1. The strategic picture

The product is currently sold out — and that shapes the whole month.

With no stock to sell today, running "buy now" ads would pay to send people to a page they can't purchase from. So the smart move is to spend this month building a waitlist and a warm audience, using the product's novelty and demand as an advantage — then switch to a focused sales push the moment stock is back. The plan is built in two phases for exactly this reason.

What's working in our favour

  • A genuinely novel product — most Indian buyers have never seen an ironing machine like this. Novelty earns cheap, organic reach.
  • A strong price story already live (₹19,999 from ₹24,999, 5-star, 30-day guarantee).
  • A premium, cohesive brand look across the feed and site.

What we need to fix

  • The "magic" — hang a damp shirt, walk away, return to a dried & ironed shirt — is barely shown. That demo is the product.
  • The feed is mood-led; light on demo, proof, and the "how does it work?" education a new category needs.
  • Sold-out status is a missed opportunity — it should be used as proof of demand, not hidden.

2. Objective & the two phases

Phase A — while sold out: maximise a warm, reachable audience. Grow the waitlist, build video-viewer and engager pools, and turn "sold out" into scarcity and social proof.

Phase B — restock launch: flip to sales. Give the waitlist early access first, then broaden to cold. This is where the revenue and return on ad spend come from.

Phase A · Capture

Sold out

Video views + waitlist lead-gen + retargeting-to-signup. Success = waitlist size & warm-pool growth.

Phase B · Convert

Back in stock

Waitlist early access → conversions → retargeting-to-purchase. Success = sales, ROAS, launch-day revenue.
