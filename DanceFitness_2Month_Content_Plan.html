✓ Do

  • Keep experience content authentic, not salesy.
  • Lead with confidence, energy, consistency, enjoyment.
  • Let the audience self-identify before the ask.

✕ Don't

  • Don't attack gyms or other workouts.
  • Don't make unrealistic body-transformation claims.
  • Don't push conversion before trust is built.
