1 · Brand & product analysis

What we're actually selling — and why the sale is a considered purchase, not an impulse buy. This shapes everything downstream.

The product, in one line

A machine that takes a damp shirt and returns it dry and ironed in ~8–12 minutes, using an adjustable inflating "shirt balloon" that presses the garment from the inside. It replaces two chores (drying + ironing) and one recurring cost (laundry/dry-clean/ironing help).

Positioning guardrail: this is not a handheld garment steamer. Never let the creative frame it as one — that's a ₹1,500 mental category and it kills the ₹19,999 price.

Why buyers hesitate (the real objections)

  • Price shock — "₹19,999 for ironing?"
  • Belief gap — "Does it really work on my shirts?"
  • Fabric fear — "Will it damage delicate clothes?"
  • Trust — new brand, high ticket, online-only
  • Deferral — "I'll think about it" (never returns)

Every content and ad angle below exists to dismantle one of these five. That is the whole game at ₹19,999.

Strategic implication for a sales-only mandate

High ticket

A ₹19,999 purchase is rarely first-touch. Expect a consideration window — proof and objection-handling matter more than reach.

Demo-led

The product sells itself on video. Seeing the damp-to-crisp transformation does more than any adjective. Demo is your hero format.

Retargeting-heavy

Most sales close on the 2nd–5th touch. The retargeting layer isn't optional here — it's where the money is made.

2 · Sales funnel thesis

One continuous machine, not four separate efforts. Each stage feeds the next and hands warm audiences down the line.

Consideration

Reach people showing intent signals (home appliance shoppers, busy professionals, new-home movers) and make them believe the machine works. Job: create video-viewers and page-engagers = tomorrow's retargeting pool.

Success = cheap ThruPlays, growing engaged audiences, rising ViewContent.

Conversion

Push warm-but-cold prospects to the product page with features, comparisons and cost-per-use math. Job: turn belief into Add-to-Cart and Initiate-Checkout.

Success = ATC/IC volume rising, landing-page views converting.

Sales

Close with social proof + offer + urgency. Real reviews, testimonials, warranty confidence, and a reason to buy now. Job: purchases.

Success = Purchases at a ROAS your margin can sustain.

Retargeting

Re-approach everyone who watched, clicked, viewed, or abandoned — with the exact objection they likely stalled on. Job: recover the deferred sale.

Success = highest ROAS in the account; lowest CPA.

Budget follows heat. As spend scales, a bigger share moves to Conversion and Retargeting, because that's where a rupee returns the most. See the budget plans in §8.

4 · Meta Ads plan — 3 campaigns

Structured as one funnel: prospect cold → convert warm-cold → close warm. All three run the Meta Sales objective where volume allows; the Consideration campaign may optimize for an upper event (ThruPlay / ViewContent) early, then shift to purchase as data builds.

Data-volume reality: at a ₹19,999 AOV, purchase events will be sparse at first. Until the pixel sees enough purchases weekly, optimize the Consideration campaign for ThruPlay or ViewContent, and the Conversion campaign for Add to Cart, then graduate to Purchase optimization once volume supports it. Don't force purchase-optimization on a starved pixel.

8 · Budget plans

Three daily levels. The honest truth up front: at a ₹19,999 AOV, small budgets buy learning and pool-building, not a flood of sales. Splits below assume the account is past the learning phase; on a cold start, weight everything toward Consideration + Retargeting for the first 2–3 weeks, then rebalance toward Conversion as the pixel matures.

₹500/day · ~₹15k/mo

Lean test. Goal: build audiences + 1–3 high-intent sales/month, not volume.

  • Consideration 40% — ₹200 (1 hero demo, broad)
  • Retargeting 60% — ₹300 (video-viewers + ATC/IC)
  • Skip a separate Conversion campaign — fold into Consideration until pools are warm

Run 1 CBO/Advantage+ campaign to avoid spreading ₹500 too thin. Judge on cost-per-ViewContent + retargeting ROAS, not raw purchase count.

₹1,000/day · ~₹30k/mo

The realistic starting point for a considered-purchase D2C. Full funnel, lightly staffed.

  • Consideration 35% — ₹350
  • Conversion 40% — ₹400 (LAL + winning interests)
  • Retargeting 25% — ₹250 (tiered warm pools)

Enough to generate ATC/IC events weekly and start feeding Lookalikes. First real read on blended ROAS by ~day 14.

₹2,000/day · ~₹60k/mo

Scale posture. Three live campaigns, room for creative testing in parallel.

  • Consideration 30% — ₹600
  • Conversion 45% — ₹900 (primary scaling lever)
  • Retargeting 25% — ₹500 (uncap dynamically if ROAS holds)

Reserve a slice of Conversion budget for a dedicated testing ad set (see §9). Scale winners 20–30% every 3–4 days, not overnight.

Golden rule across all three: never let retargeting starve. A small, always-on retargeting spend against warm ATC/IC pools is almost always the highest-ROAS rupee you spend — protect it first, scale prospecting second.

9 · Creative testing plan

Test to learn what sells, not to admire creatives. Isolate one variable at a time so a winner tells you why it won.

Testing ladder (in order)

  • 1 · Angle — demo vs value-math vs testimonial vs comparison. Find the winning message first; it matters most.
  • 2 · Hook — same winning angle, 3 different first-3-seconds. Hook decides hold-rate.
  • 3 · Format — Reel vs carousel vs static for the winning angle.
  • 4 · Offer/CTA — "Shop Now" vs "Learn More"; offer-led vs benefit-led copy.

How to run it cleanly

  • Dedicated testing ad set (or Advantage+ with clearly named creatives) so tests don't disturb proven ad sets.
  • 3–4 creatives per test, one variable different. Name them so results are readable.
  • Let each run to a meaningful signal — roughly ~1,000+ impressions or a few days, and ideally some ATC before you judge. Don't kill on 200 impressions.
  • Primary decision metric: cost per ATC / ViewContent early; ROAS once purchases accrue. Hold-rate & CTR are diagnostic, not verdicts.
  • Winners graduate into Conversion/Retargeting; losers are paused, learnings logged.
Sample-size honesty: at low budgets, purchase data is thin, so early "winners" can be noise. Lean on upper-funnel proxies (hold-rate, cost-per-ViewContent, cost-per-ATC) to decide faster, and confirm with ROAS once enough purchases land. Treat any single-test conclusion as provisional until it repeats.

10 · Weekly optimization plan

A fixed rhythm beats reactive tinkering. Avoid daily knee-jerk edits — they reset learning. Here's the weekly loop.

DayFocusActions
MonRead the weekPull last-7-day report: spend, ATC, IC, purchases, ROAS, CPA, frequency by campaign. Note what moved and why.
TueCreative healthCheck frequency & hold-rate. Pause creatives with rising frequency (>~2.5–3 on retargeting) or falling CTR/hold. Queue replacements from the test winners.
WedAudience & funnelRefresh retargeting pools (they update automatically, but check exclusions). Confirm ATC→IC→Purchase drop-off; if checkout leaks, flag the page, not the ad.
ThuBudget shiftMove budget toward the best CPA/ROAS ad sets. Scale winners 20–30% max; don't double overnight. Protect retargeting spend.
FriLaunch next testsPush new creative test live (one variable, §9). Prep weekend offer/urgency creative for warm pools if an offer is running.
WkndLight-touch monitorWatch for spend anomalies & disapprovals only. No structural edits over the weekend.

Escalation triggers (act immediately)

  • CPA drifting above break-even for 3+ days → pause weakest ad set, reallocate
  • Frequency high + CTR dropping → creative fatigue, swap now
  • Clicks high but no ATC → landing-page/price problem, not ad problem
  • ATC high but no purchase → checkout friction (COD? shipping cost? trust?)

Leave-it-alone rules

  • Don't edit ad sets still in learning (resets it)
  • Don't judge purchase performance on <3–5 days of data
  • Don't scale on one good day — scale on a good week
  • Don't add 6 creatives at once to a small budget — starves each

11 · Pre-launch tracking checklist

None of the above works if the events aren't firing. Confirm this before a single rupee goes out — this is the foundation the whole sales machine sits on.

  • Meta Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI) live and de-duplicated on the website
  • Standard events firing correctly: ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase (with value + currency)
  • Product catalog connected (enables dynamic/DPA cart-recovery ads)
  • Domain verified; Aggregated Event Measurement priorities set (Purchase = 1)
  • Custom Audiences from §5 created and populating
  • UTM parameters on all ad links for clean attribution
  • Confirm current price/offer and update all offer creative accordingly
  • Verify all testimonials/reviews are genuine and you have permission to use them
  • Check performance/time claims are substantiable (ASCI) before publishing ads
