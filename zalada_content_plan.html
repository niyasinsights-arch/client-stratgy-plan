Zalada Content Plan

Part 1 – AI Videos

Video 01 – From Trivandrum to Kochi

Visual Idea

  1. Start with an aerial view of Trivandrum city, gradually moving towards Infopark.
  2. Show a Zalada vending machine inside Infopark, highlighting its existing presence.
  3. A digital map of Kerala appears, marking Trivandrum and Kochi.
  4. A Zalada-branded vehicle begins its journey from Trivandrum towards Kochi.
  5. Show glimpses of Kerala highways and the changing city landscapes.
  6. The vehicle reaches Kochi and moves towards Lulu Mall.
  7. End with a reveal of the Zalada vending machine inside Lulu Mall, showcasing its food options.

Video 02 – When Hunger Finds You

Visual Idea

  1. Start with a young professional working on a laptop at an office desk.
  2. After working for some time, the person suddenly feels hungry and decides to take a short break.
  3. The person gets up from the desk and walks towards the Zalada vending machine.
  4. Show the person exploring the available food options on the machine.
  5. The person selects a preferred product and completes the purchase.
  6. Show the selected product being dispensed from the vending machine.
  7. The person takes the product and walks towards a nearby table.
  8. Show the person sitting comfortably and enjoying the food.
  9. End with a close-up of the product alongside the Zalada vending machine.

Video 03 – From Fresh Ingredients to a Perfect Bowl

Visual Idea

  1. Start with fresh ingredients arranged on a kitchen counter – lettuce, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, carrots and bell peppers.
  2. Show vegetables being washed under running water in slow motion.
  3. Show close-up shots of vegetables being chopped and prepared.
  4. An empty salad bowl appears, and the ingredients gradually float into it, forming colourful layers.
  5. Show dressing being poured over the salad in slow motion.
  6. Reveal the finished, colourful salad bowl.
  7. Transition the prepared salad into Zalada's packaged salad bowl.
  8. End with the Zalada vending machine and the finished product.

Part 2 – Real Videos

Video 04 – Kitchen Inauguration & Showcase

Capture the inauguration of Zalada's new kitchen, highlighting the opening moments, ribbon-cutting ceremony, guests and team members. Along with the inauguration, showcase the kitchen interiors, equipment, food preparation areas and overall setup. The video introduces the new kitchen and marks an important milestone in Zalada's growth.

Video 05 – From Kitchen to Customer

Show the complete journey of Zalada products, starting from food preparation and packaging to transportation and stocking inside the vending machine. End with a customer purchasing a product. This video highlights how food reaches customers through Zalada's vending system.

Video 06 – The Art of Food Preparation

Capture the chef preparing Zalada's food items, from handling fresh ingredients to the final presentation. Focus on preparation techniques, ingredients and attention to detail to make the food visually appealing.

Video 07 – Kochi, We're Here!

Capture the inauguration of Zalada's vending machine at Lulu Mall, Kochi. Include the inauguration moments, guests, first purchases and customer interactions. The video announces Zalada's arrival in Kochi.

Video 08 – How to Order from Zalada

Create a simple demonstration showing how customers can purchase products from the Zalada vending machine. Cover browsing the available products, selecting an item, completing the payment and collecting the product. The objective is to make the ordering process easy to understand.
