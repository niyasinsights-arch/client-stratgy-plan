Overview

Objective: Position Zalada as a convenient, modern and healthy food brand for Gen Z, working professionals, Infopark employees, interns and health-conscious adults.

The strategy is not just to introduce healthy food products. It is to build curiosity, encourage first-time purchases, create healthy eating habits and convert first-time customers into regular, loyal customers.

Primary target audience

  • Gen Z
  • College students and interns
  • Infopark employees and IT professionals
  • Working professionals
  • Health-conscious adults
  • People interested in fitness, nutrition and healthy eating

2. Customer loyalty and credit point strategy

Objective: Encourage repeat purchases by rewarding customers for consistently choosing healthier food from Zalada, through a website-based registration and credit point system.

Customer journey

1. Register→ 2. Purchase→ 3. Earn→ 4. Track→ 5. Achieve→ 6. Redeem
StepWhat happens
1. RegisterCustomer creates an account on the Zalada website
2. PurchaseCustomer buys healthy food from Zalada
3. EarnCredit points are added based on qualifying purchases and food choices
4. TrackCustomer monitors monthly progress through their account
5. AchieveCustomer reaches the monthly credit point target
6. RedeemCustomer unlocks additional benefits and rewards

A. Customer registration

  • Customers register through the Zalada website.
  • Basic customer information is collected with their consent.
  • Each registered customer receives a unique account or ID.
  • Purchases and earned credit points are tracked through the website.
  • Customers can monitor their progress and rewards through their accounts.

B. Healthy food credit point system

Customers earn points based on their purchases and participation in Zalada's healthy eating programme. The system can consider:

  • Protein content of purchased meals
  • Fibre content
  • Nutritional balance
  • Healthier meal selections
  • Frequency of purchases
  • Consistency in choosing nutritious options

For example, points for selected protein-rich meals, balanced food combinations or other qualifying menu items. The system should reward balanced choices, not high protein or low fat in isolation.

C. Monthly achievement and rewards

  • Monthly achievement targets are introduced.
  • Customers collect points through the month, tracked on the website.
  • Customers who hit the monthly target unlock additional rewards.
  • Rewards can include discounts, complimentary food items, bonus points or exclusive offers.

D. Website integration and tracking

The Zalada website is the central platform for the loyalty programme. Required features:

  • Customer registration and login
  • Customer profile management
  • Purchase history
  • Credit point tracking
  • Monthly achievement dashboard
  • Reward and redemption system
  • Notifications about new offers and achievements
  • Customer progress reports

Expected outcome: A long-term relationship with customers, by making healthy food purchases rewarding and encouraging consistent engagement with the brand.

3. Community engagement and fitness challenges

Objective: Build an active, health-conscious community around Zalada by connecting food, fitness and everyday lifestyle habits. Engagement goes beyond food purchases to simple health and fitness challenges.

A. Community formation

Health-focused communities on WhatsApp, Instagram or other suitable channels, for:

  • Infopark employees
  • IT professionals
  • College students and interns
  • Fitness enthusiasts
  • Walking and running groups
  • Health-conscious individuals

B. Daily and weekly fitness challenges

  • 5K Steps Challenge: Participants who complete 5,000 steps in a day become eligible for a predefined Zalada reward, such as 25% off eligible menu items.
  • 10K Steps Challenge: Participants who complete 10,000 steps unlock a higher-level reward or additional benefits.

Other potential challenges: 7-day healthy eating, weekly walking, daily hydration, healthy breakfast, no-junk-food, 30-day wellness.

C. Reward and discount mechanism (illustrative)

AchievementSuggested reward
5,000 steps25% discount
10,000 stepsHigher-tier reward
7-day challengeBonus credit points
30-day challengeExclusive rewards

The exact discount structure, eligibility, redemption limits and reward values should be finalised based on Zalada's margins and business objectives.

D. Challenge tracking and verification

  1. Participants join through a registration link.
  2. Daily challenges are announced through the community.
  3. Participants submit step-count screenshots or use an approved activity-tracking integration.
  4. Completed challenges are verified.
  5. Eligible participants receive discount codes or reward credits.
  6. Weekly winners and achievements are featured on Zalada's social media.

E. Community engagement content

  • Weekly challenge announcements
  • Participant achievement highlights
  • Community leaderboards
  • Fitness influencer collaborations
  • User-generated challenge videos
  • Monthly winner announcements
  • Customer transformation and experience stories

Expected outcome: A community that associates Zalada with healthy living, daily movement and positive lifestyle choices, not only a food vending machine.

Final strategic vision

Don't just sell healthy food. Build a healthy lifestyle around the brand. The content strategy creates awareness and curiosity. The food experience converts curiosity into first-time purchases. The credit point system encourages repeat purchases, while community challenges create engagement beyond the product itself. Together, they aim to transform Zalada from a healthy vending machine into an everyday healthy lifestyle brand.
