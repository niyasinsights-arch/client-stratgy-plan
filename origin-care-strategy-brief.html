Q2

Priority customer segments & revenue model

Five buyer segments, ranked by their likely contribution to revenue (which differs from share of audience — a premium soap brand earns disproportionately from gifting and diaspora baskets).

SegmentEst. share of revenueConfidence
Conscious urban women, 28–45Kerala metros & Tier-2 — Kochi, Trivandrum, Calicut, Thrissur. Label-readers, skin-sensitive, already buying "natural." 30–35% High
Gifting & premium-hamper buyersOnam, weddings, corporate gifting. Buy in multiples — highest basket value. ~20% Medium
NRI / Gulf-Malayali householdsBuying for family in Kerala or shipping abroad. Strong pull toward authentic Kerala Ayurveda. 15–20% Medium
Skin-condition seekersEczema, acne, sensitive & baby skin. Switching away from chemical soaps. ~15% Medium
Pan-India clean-beauty shoppersDiscover via Instagram & marketplaces. Buy the hero bar first. 10–15% Lower
Where the volume is. Segment 1 is the steady, repeat-purchase base with subscription potential. Segments 2 and 3 are the basket-size amplifiers — fewer buyers, larger orders. If the goal is unit volume rather than margin, gifting and sensitive-skin problem-solvers convert fastest, because both buy on emotion and urgency rather than price.
Q3

Competitive landscape

Grouped by where they compete with Origin. The local cottage cluster is fragmented and under-branded — which is precisely Origin's opening.

Inside Kerala Home market

  • Medimix — Ayurvedic/herbal soap, Thrissur origins. Mass-market; competes on the Ayurvedic claim, not premium-handcrafted.
  • Chandrika — long-standing Kerala Ayurvedic soap, mass-premium heritage brand.
  • Kerala Soaps (KSIE) — state-owned legacy brands (Kerala Sandal). Heritage, not premium D2C.
  • Regional handmade makers — a wide cluster of small Kozhikode / Malappuram / Trivandrum cold-process makers. Direct rivals, but small and weakly branded.

Outside Kerala National / D2C

  • Vilvah — Coimbatore D2C natural skincare & soap. Strong social-led brand.
  • Bare Necessities — Bangalore, plant-based zero-waste handcrafted soaps. Closest positioning rival.
  • Rustic Art · Soap Opera · Khadi Naturals — national handmade / organic soap names.
  • Kama Ayurveda · Forest Essentials — premium Ayurvedic skincare houses. Not soap-first, but they own the "luxury Ayurveda" mental space and set the price ceiling.
The gap to own. The Ayurvedic-soap category below is crowded and price-driven; the premium luxury-Ayurveda space above is skincare, not soap. Origin's room to win is the premium, handcrafted, Kerala-botanical bar — a space local makers are too small to claim and national brands don't lead with.
Q4

Digital marketing signals

Keywords, targeting, behaviours, pain points, intents and interests to build campaigns around.

Keywords & search terms

  • Ayurvedic soap · herbal soap · handmade soap · organic soap · chemical-free soap
  • Kerala soap · botanical bath care · cold-process soap · sandalwood / neem / turmeric soap
  • Soap for sensitive skin / acne / eczema / dry skin · baby-safe natural soap
  • Long-tail: "best natural soap in Kerala," "chemical free soap online India"

Meta targeting & interests

  • Interests: Ayurveda, organic beauty, clean beauty, natural skincare, sustainable living, handmade products
  • Brand-affinity: Forest Essentials, Kama Ayurveda, Khadi, Biotique, The Body Shop
  • Behaviours: engaged shoppers, online purchasers
  • NRI layer: "away from hometown Kerala" + Malayalam language for the Gulf-Malayali segment

Behaviours to build around

  • Ingredient-label reading & switching away from chemical soap
  • Gifting cycles — Onam, weddings, Diwali, corporate
  • Seasonal skin flare-ups — monsoon skin issues are a real Kerala hook

Pain points to message

  • Chemical soaps causing dryness, irritation, allergies
  • Distrust of "natural" labels that aren't genuinely natural
  • Wanting authentic Ayurveda without the luxury price tag
  • Sensitive & baby skin needing a safe, gentle bar

Search intents — map content to the funnel

Problem-aware — "soap for eczema / sensitive skin" → educational + product-solution content

Solution-aware — "handmade Ayurvedic soap online" → hero-product & ingredient storytelling

Comparison — "best organic soap brand Kerala" → proof, reviews, brand differentiation

Gifting — "natural soap gift set / hamper" → bundle & seasonal offers

Q6

Positioning recommendation

Not all four attributes flat — that dilutes into "generic natural soap." Stack them with a clear lead.

Lead
Botanical  Freshest, most premium space. Sidesteps the crowded mass-market "Ayurvedic soap" category.
Proof
Kerala heritage  The authenticity moat — why NRI & heritage-gifting buyers trust the brand. Tell it as a story, not the headline.
Credibility
Ayurvedic  Gives efficacy credibility, but it's the most commoditised claim — a supporting pillar, not the flag.
Texture
Traditional / handcrafted  The sensory, craft layer — powers reels and packaging narrative. Weak as a standalone position.
"Kerala's first botanical bath care, rooted in Ayurvedic wisdom — handcrafted." Premium, defensible, and clearly separated from the ₹25–40 mass Ayurvedic-soap market where price wins.
One thing to substantiate. The "Kerala's First" claim is a strong assertion. Confirm it is genuinely defensible before scaling it in paid campaigns — if a competitor challenges it, it undercuts brand trust.
