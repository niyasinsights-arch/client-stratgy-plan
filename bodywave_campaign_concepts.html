Campaign 01Reel · Field activation

Public Fitness Campaign

Take Body Wave off the gym floor and into the community — a live fitness activation at nearby women's colleges or busy public spots. Invite people into a quick challenge, put the trainers front and centre, and turn genuine reactions into a high-energy reel that positions the gym as active, local, and welcoming.

Objectives
  • Increase local brand awareness in Calicut
  • Engage college students and young adults directly
  • Generate social-media engagement from authentic footage
  • Drive membership enquiries via a time-boxed student offer
Execution
  1. Pick 1–2 nearby women's colleges or busy public spaces and secure permission to set up.
  2. Set a simple, inclusive challenge anyone can try in 30–60 seconds (e.g. plank hold, squat count, a quick trainer-led combo).
  3. Feature female trainers motivating participants and dropping one quick, useful fitness tip each.
  4. Offer participants an exclusive 20% student discount on the spot.
  5. Capture authentic reactions and cut them into one punchy reel + a Stories set.
The offer

Exclusive 20% student discount for participants. Set a clear expiry (e.g. valid 2 weeks) and a simple redemption (show the reel / a code / a flyer at reception) so it drives action while it's fresh.

Content outputs
  • 1 hero reel (reactions + trainer energy)
  • Stories set — live, day-of
  • Optional carousel of the best moments
  • Clips banked for future reels
Success signals
  • Reel reach, shares & saves
  • New followers in the days after
  • Discount redemptions / enquiries
  • Comments & profile visits
Watch-outs
  • Permissions: get institutional sign-off for any campus, and a permit if a public space requires one.
  • Consent: as a women-only brand, get explicit filming consent from every participant; offer to keep anyone who prefers off-camera or blurred.
  • Timing: many Kerala colleges break for Onam in late August — worth checking term dates and running the campus activation before the break or after reopening, not during.
  • Claims: promote only the real discount and real terms; don't imply results or offers you won't honour.

Campaign 02Reel · UGC challenge

Easy Gym Fitness Challenge

A fun, low-barrier fitness challenge — inspired by the reference reel — that members and non-members can attempt, tag Body Wave in, and pass to their friends. Built to spread through nominations and generate a steady stream of user-generated content.

Objectives
  • Increase brand awareness through shares and nominations
  • Boost community engagement
  • Attract new members
  • Generate a bank of user-generated content (UGC)
Execution
  1. Define one simple, repeatable challenge task and a branded hashtag (e.g. #BodyWaveChallenge) so entries are easy to find.
  2. Seed it first — trainers and a few members model the challenge so others know exactly what to do.
  3. Record participants attempting it, with trainers motivating and guiding.
  4. Ask each participant to nominate 2–3 friends to keep the chain going.
  5. Collect entries over a set window, then announce winners.
Rewards

Prizes for winners — e.g. a free one-week pass, a discounted membership, or fitness merchandise. Publish clear, simple rules up front: how to enter, the deadline, and how winners are chosen.

Mechanic & timeline
  • Entry: do the task, tag the gym, use the hashtag, nominate 2–3
  • Run window: ~2 weeks
  • Winner announcement: reel + Stories
  • Judging: energy / creativity / reach (your call)
Success signals
  • Number of entries & unique participants
  • Hashtag uses & nominations
  • Reach from reposted UGC
  • New followers & enquiries
Watch-outs
  • UGC rights: get each participant's permission before reposting their clip to the gym's page.
  • Consent & safety: women-only brand — keep entries comfortable, never pressure anyone on camera, and keep the task genuinely easy so no one risks injury.
  • Rules clarity: state the deadline, prize terms and how winners are picked up front to avoid disputes.
  • Seeding: a challenge with zero early entries stalls — line up the first 5–10 before promoting it widely.
