How to run this plan

Each week pairs feed posts (tap any card for the full brief) with a Stories & Extras layer — polls, Q&A, reposts, and the soft UGC seed that becomes month two's first customer content. Two feed posts a week keeps a premium account unhurried; the stories layer carries daily engagement in between.

Before you publish

  • Keep skincare claims soft — describe feel and ingredients, not clinical outcomes.
  • Verify the 1914 Calicut heritage date and any sourcing claims against a real source.
  • Use only genuine, consented testimonials, father's-story details, and maker names.
  • Hold one signature closing frame across every reel for brand recall.
