About CelluPro Green

Cellupro Green Private Limited (CPG) produces high quality micro/crystalline and nanocellulose pulp from agricultural waste. Our raw materials are agro residues such as rice straw, sugarcane bagasse and pineapple straw, chosen for their seasonal availability and affordable price.

The pulp is a base material for cosmetics such as creams, lotions, concealers, foundations, face masks and sunscreens. It also goes into paper products such as sustainable packaging and sanitary napkins. Researchers can buy high-purity nanocellulose to develop new biocomposites, and we also supply lignin (alkali wash) for varied uses.

Our vision

To lead the global transition toward renewable, biodegradable material systems, where every agricultural residue becomes a valuable resource powering a circular, low-carbon future.

Our mission

To convert agro residues into high quality micro and nanocellulose materials through science-driven innovation, enabling sustainable products that improve human well-being and reduce environmental impact.

How it is made

1

Collect

Agro residues such as rice straw, sugarcane bagasse and pineapple straw.

2

Extract

Cellulose is separated from the residue.

3

Refine

Reduced to micro and nano scale in our lab.

4

Use

Supplied as Ecagro pulp, or formulated into Celluglitz skincare.
