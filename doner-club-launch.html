Campaign overview

Campaign objective

Introduce Doner Club to the Hyderabad market, build curiosity around the new outlet, establish product awareness and get people through the door to experience the brand.

Target audience

Young adults 18–35 College students Young professionals Food enthusiasts Friends & families, casual dining International & QSR fans

Key communication objectives

  • Create awareness about Doner Club’s arrival in Hyderabad.
  • Introduce Döner as a distinctive food experience.
  • Showcase products, preparation and restaurant ambience.
  • Build trust through genuine customer experiences.
  • Encourage first-time visits and repeat purchases.

Marketing approach

Six stages, run in order. Each post in the sequence below belongs to one of them.

Stage 1

Awareness

AI Coming Soon video and launch announcement.

Stage 2

Brand intro

Inauguration, outlet walkthrough and brand identity.

Stage 3

Product discovery

Döner preparation, hero products and ingredients.

Stage 4

Engagement

AI storytelling, friends’ experiences, customer reactions.

Stage 5

Conversion

Opening offers, menu promotions, location-based CTAs.

Stage 6

Community

Customer-generated content, reviews and repeat visits.
