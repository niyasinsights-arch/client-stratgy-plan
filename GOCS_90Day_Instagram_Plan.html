Kerala Heritage Snacks — Digital Growth Strategy

GOCS

90-Day Instagram Content & Sales Marketing Plan

Strategic Foundation

Objective & Commercial Funnel

Move GOCS from a small regional snack account to a recognisable Kerala savoury brand — built on authenticity, nostalgia, product variety, gifting, social proof and modern short-form storytelling.

1
Discovery Reach new Malayali, Kerala-food and traditional-snack audiences.
2
Consideration Explain products and prove freshness, hygiene, quality and authenticity.
3
Conversion Drive website visits, WhatsApp enquiries, first orders, bundles and gifting purchases.
4
Retention Encourage reviews, repeat orders, referrals and customer-generated content.

Publishing Rhythm — 14 Posts / Month

5 ReelsReach & product desire
3 CarouselsProduct education & consideration
3 ConversionBundles, offers & gifting
2 TestimonialTrust & community
1 FounderAuthenticity & cultural story

Content Rules

  • Show the product within the first 2 seconds of every Reel.
  • Every sales post — clear product name and purchase action.
  • Consistent visual treatment across product, bundle and testimonial covers.
  • One primary action per post: shop, WhatsApp, save, comment, share, review or reorder.
  • Pin best product education post, bestseller and ordering guide.
  • Track clicks, enquiries, add-to-cart, purchases, saves, shares and repeat orders.
  • Malayalam + English together; subtitles on every Reel.
